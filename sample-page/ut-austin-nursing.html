Nursing at UT Austin: Graduate Earnings, Debt, and Outcomes

Bachelor's degree in Registered NursingPublic university, Austin, TXAbout 118 graduates a year
Data: College Scorecard release of June 2026 · BLS wages May 2025
The short answer Computed + generated text

UT Austin nursing graduates earn about what nursing graduates earn nationally, $75,095 in their first year. Where this program stands out is debt: the typical graduate leaves with $19,651 in federal loans, less than at 90% of nursing programs in the country. Strong value, but not one of the highest-paying nursing degrees in Texas.

Where graduates work, and what the pay is worth ScorecardBEACalc

87% of graduates (121 of 139) were working in Texas one year after graduating. Prices in the Austin area are about 2% below the national average, so a $75,095 salary here buys what about $76,576 buys in a typical US city.

The same adjustment helps Texas nurses overall. The median Texas RN earns $95,970, slightly less than the national median of $97,550. After adjusting for Texas prices, that pay is worth about $98,880, which puts Texas slightly ahead.

Common questions Generated from data

How much do UT Austin nursing graduates make?

The median graduate earns $75,095 one year after graduating and $82,838 five years after. Registered nurses in the Austin area earn a median of $97,890 across all experience levels.

How much debt do UT Austin nursing graduates have?

The median federal loan balance is $19,651, which is the 4th lowest of 53 nursing bachelor's programs in Texas. That works out to about $208 a month over 10 years.

Is a nursing degree from UT Austin worth it?

On the numbers, yes for most students. Debt is low compared to pay, 26% of first-year earnings against a national median of 37%, and the default rate two years out is 10% or less. If the highest possible early salary is the main goal, several Texas programs report higher pay, usually with more debt.

Do UT Austin nursing graduates stay in Texas?

Most do. 87% were working in Texas one year after graduating.

About this data

  • Earnings and debt only include students who received federal financial aid. At UT Austin that covers most but not all nursing graduates.
  • Earnings only count graduates who were working and not enrolled in more school. Graduates who went straight to a master's program are not included.
  • Debt only includes federal loans. Private loans and credit cards are not counted.
  • Scorecard figures describe graduating classes from several years ago. BLS wages are from May 2025.
  • School-level numbers like admission rate and graduation rate are for the whole university, not the nursing program.
